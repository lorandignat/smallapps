# QA reports

Each task is verified against its acceptance criteria and written up in
`TASK-###-review.md`. Those transcripts quote the build host, its paths and the
signing account, so they stay in the private archive. The verdict, its date and
any findings are recorded on the board in `backlog/BOARD.md`; the standing
checks live in `TEST-PLAN.md` and `REGRESSION.md`.
